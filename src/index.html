Your private, offline journal and task manager with a local AI partner. Everything stays on this machine.

Bring your own models — nothing is downloaded. Arnold needs a local llama-server runtime and two GGUF files you pick yourself:

  • llama-server.exe — bundled in the Windows release; from source, stage your own llama.cpp build
  • A chat GGUF — any instruct-tuned model works; Qwythos-9B is what the prompts and tools are tuned against
  • An embedding GGUF — used only to index and search journal entries (nomic-embed recommended)

No account, no cloud, no network. The AI can never write to your data on its own — every change becomes a proposal you accept in the Review Inbox.

  • Write journal entries - the AI reads them automatically
  • Tasks get extracted and managed through conversation
  • 100% local. No accounts, no cloud, no internet required

Arnold bundles no model runtime. Point the app at your llama-server.exe (from a llama.cpp build) and it will manage the servers for you.
