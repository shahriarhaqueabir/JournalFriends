Arnold

Your private thinking partner, strictly offline.

The Mission

In an era of constant connectivity and cloud-first everything, Arnold is built on a different principle: Your thoughts should remain yours. Our mission is to provide a world-class productivity tool that leverages the power of AI without ever compromising your privacy.

🛡️

True Privacy

No accounts, no trackers, and no data collection. Your journals never leave your machine.

🔌

Offline-First

Everything works without an internet connection. Your data is stored in a local SQLite database.

🤖

Local AI

Powered by llama.cpp. Analysis and chat happen entirely on your own hardware.

Technical Stack

  • Shell: Tauri v2 (Rust + WebView2)
  • Database: SQLite with Full-Text Search
  • AI Engine: llama.cpp (any instruct-tuned GGUF; Qwythos recommended)
  • UI: Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS (No frameworks)
